# EQLSaK Character Build Advisor privacy notice

Last updated: September 30, 2026

EQLSaK Character Build Advisor is an offline Windows companion for EverQuest Legends players. It has no user account, activation system, cloud AI model, advertising system, or analytics service.

## Information the app can use on your device

The app can read files only when you choose them or use a local discovery tool. These files can include game combat logs, loot logs, guild dumps, and map files. Those files may contain character names, guild names, chat text, game events, or locations.

The app saves its settings, imported files, build plans, and reports on your device. It does not send those items to the EQLSaK project, OpenAI, ChatGPT, Daybreak Game Company, Microsoft, or another service.

## Optional online actions

The app can open links to outside sites and can check rule-source pages only when you choose that action. Those sites receive the normal technical information that a browser sends when you visit them, such as your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy rules apply.

If you use an optional donation link after one is configured, payment is handled by the named donation provider. EQLSaK does not collect or process payment-card or bank information. Donations are voluntary and do not unlock app features.

## Sharing and sales

EQLSaK does not sell, rent, trade, or use your local game data for advertising. The project does not receive a copy of your logs, maps, guild dump, faction notes, build plans, or combat reports.

## Your choices

You choose whether to import or watch a local file, open an outside link, or run an optional source check. You can remove EQLSaK's local app data by uninstalling the app and deleting any saved files you chose to keep.

## Contact

For privacy questions or to report a problem, use the project's [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/venducati/eqlsak-build-advisor/issues). Do not post real player chat, complete logs, payment information, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

## Independence notice

EQLSaK is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Daybreak Game Company, EverQuest Legends, OpenAI, or ChatGPT.
